Welcome to CookBook: Recipe Book & Planner, an elegant, full-stack culinary companion designed to streamline recipe management, weekly meal planning, and grocery shopping.
Crafted with a clean, high-contrast user interface styled using premium emerald accents and generous negative space, the application provides a highly responsive system for organizing recipes with beautiful, fluid motion transitions.

Core Applications & Features:

1. Recipe Discovery & Filtering
Dynamic Category Filters: Smoothly browse culinary entries categorized across breakfast, lunch, dinner, dessert, and snack course sections. Transitions between these categories utilize hardware-accelerated fluid motion scales and fades to avoid layout jumps or visual stutters.
On-Demand Search: Instantly filter recipes by title, keywords, ingredients, or descriptive tags.
Tag Explorer: Quickly isolate recipes by style or dietary preference (e.g., Vegetarian, Quick Prep, Gluten-Free) via an interactive tag cloud.

2. Comprehensive Meal Planner
Weekly Timetables: Map out your culinary schedule day-by-day across breakfast, lunch, and dinner slots.
Macro & Detail Integration: Review active ingredient bounds, preparatory steps, and cooking times directly from mapped planner intervals.

3. Intelligent Shopping List & Curated Groceries
Auto-Consolidated Ingredients: Automatically aggregate all ingredients across your scheduled weekly meal plans.
Custom Grocery Lists: Manually add independent grocery items, adjust checkmarks, and track required kitchen supplies.

4. NutriCare Analytics
Nutritional Overviews: Track micro and macronutrient breakdowns dynamically to align with wellness diets.
Adjustable Servings & Cost: Recalculate component lists on-the-fly with integrated baseline global currency multipliers.

5. Portable Local Storage & Database Portability
Secure Raw Exports: Take complete custody of custom culinary data by downloading local backups or copying the raw metadata straight to your clipboard.
Backup Recovery: Easily restore previously saved timetables, custom dinner courses, and curated groceries by uploading your raw local JSON database snapshots.
